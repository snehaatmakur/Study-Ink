How to practice
  1. Write in an underlined blank or the redraw area.
  2. Press Check: Claude reads your ink (answers are graded in code, never by the model), or type what you wrote.
  3. Use Reveal for an answer and Reveal all for the full key.
  4. Export the page as a PDF or its editable JSON model.
Claude reads your handwriting and the answer is then checked in code. Put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in the .env file next to app.py and restart to enable it; otherwise type what you wrote.
